Engineering Methodology Guide

✕
Overview API 1163 Tolerance B31G Original B31G Modified RSTRENG NG-18 Screening Kastner Combined Psafe & ERF Reliability Engine CGR & Remaining Life References

00 Overview

The tool evaluates pipeline metal-loss and mechanical anomalies against their governing failure pressure (Pf), deriving safe working pressure (Psafe = Pf / SF) and Estimated Repair Factor (ERF = MAOP / Psafe). Corrosion is assessed independent of orientation (axial vs. circumferential) since the governing B31G/RSTRENG limit-load formulations are not orientation-specific; nine anomaly classifications route to five calculation engines:

1. Corrosion — B31G: ASME B31G Original and 0.85dL Modified, evaluated simultaneously from a single reported length/depth (no profile required).
2. Corrosion — RSTRENG: Effective-area method using the full River Bottom Profile (RBP); falls back to a Modified-B31G-equivalent estimate if no valid profile is supplied.
3. Axial Milling & Gouge: NG-18 Surface Flaw model (C = 1.0 rectangular profile).
4. Circumferential Milling & Gouge: Kastner Plastic Ring Collapse formulation, accounting for combined external axial tension and bending (σL,ext).
5. Mechanical Dents: ASME B31.8 / B31.4 strain and depth criteria for Plain Dents (≤ 6.0% OD), Dents on Welds (≤ 2.0% OD), and Dents with Gouge (0.0% OD / immediate repair).

01 API 1163 / POF Tool Sizing Tolerance Implementation

Per standard pipeline inspection practice (API 1163 / POF), ILI tool sizing tolerance is applied conservatively to reported anomaly depth (drep), while nominal pipe wall thickness (tnom) is preserved for structural bulging and hoop burst containment:

d_eval (d_upper)= min( t_nom, d_rep + (tolerance / 100) × t_nom )

This avoids artificial compounding penalties where wall thickness reduction would simultaneously decrease pipe burst pressure and inflate the Folias bulging parameter z = L2 / (D · t).

02 ASME B31G Original (Corrected Piecewise Formulation)

ASME B31G mandates a piecewise formulation that strictly transitions at z = L2 / (D · tnom) = 20 (equivalent to Folias factor parameter A = 4.0):

Flow stress Sf1.1 × SMYS
zL² / (D × t_nom)
For z ≤ 20M = √(1 + 0.8z), C = 2/3 (Parabolic profile)
Pf (z ≤ 20)Sf × (2t/D) × [(1 − (2/3)·d_eval/t) / (1 − (2/3)·d_eval/(t·M))]
For z > 20Pf = Sf × (2t/D) × (1 − d_eval/t)  [Uniform Depth Long Flaw Limit]
Engineering Verification: Flaws exceeding z = 20 lose curvature-dependent bulging containment and collapse as an infinitely long groove. The tool enforces this statutory threshold.

03 ASME B31G Modified (0.85dL)

Universal flow stress calculation automatically switches for high-strength steels (SMYS ≥ 550 MPa or Grade X80+):

Flow stress Sf(SMYS + UTS) / 2  if SMYS ≥ 550 MPa; else min(SMYS + 69 MPa, UTS)
zL² / (D × t_nom)
Folias factor M√(1 + 0.6275z − 0.003375z²)  if z ≤ 50;  else 0.032z + 3.3
PfSf × (2t/D) × [(1 − 0.85·d_eval/t) / (1 − 0.85·d_eval/(t·M))]

04 RSTRENG — Effective Area Method

Iterates through all possible contiguous sub-segments [i, j] (where j > i) of the River Bottom Profile (RBP) to establish the governing combination of localized depth and sub-length. Optimized with precalculated cumulative area integration. If any sub-segment exceeds critical instability (1 − (A/A0) / M ≤ 0), rupture capacity drops immediately to 0.

A/AoTrapezoidal Metal Loss Area / (Sub-Length × t_nom)
PfSf × (2t/D) × [(1 − A/Ao) / (1 − (A/Ao)/M(sub-length))]

05 NG-18 Surface Flaw (Screening)

Standard Battelle surface flaw rectangular cross-section model (C = 1.0) with calibrated Folias factor M = √(1 + 0.26 · (L2 / (D · tnom))) and empirical material stress derating penalties deducted from calculated hoop failure.

Level 1 Screening Limitation: Mechanical gouges introduce triaxial cold work, plastic strain, and micro-fissuring. Limit-load equations serve as preliminary screening only. Gouges combined with cyclic fatigue require Level 2/3 Fracture Mechanics (API 579-1 / BS 7910).

06 Kastner Plastic Collapse with Longitudinal Interaction

Assesses circumferential anomalies under plastic ring collapse, applying a deterministic depth penalty (+0.5 mm) to gouges, restricted by a statutory local width guard (W ≤ πD / 2), deducting external axial tension (σL,ext), and strictly capped by the pipe hoop stress burst limit (Phoop = (Sf − Δσgouge) · 2tnom / D).

cal Sf1.15·SMYS × [ (1−d/t)·(π − c/R) / ((1−d/t)π + 2·(d/t)·sin(c/R)) ]
Net Axialmax( 0, cal Sf − Δσ_gouge − σ_L,ext )
Pfmin( (4·t_nom·Net Axial) / D , (Sf − Δσ_gouge)·(2t_nom/D) )

07 Safe Working Pressure & ERF

PsafePf / SF  (Design Safety Factor, typically 1.25–1.39)
ERFMAOP / Psafe
Statutory DepthMandatory repair triggered when reported d_rep / t_nom ≥ 80% NWT
Repair RequiredERF > 1.00 (Immediate / Scheduled Remediation Required)
Investigation0.95 < ERF ≤ 1.00 (Detailed Engineering Investigation / Field Verification)
Monitoring0.80 ≤ ERF ≤ 0.95 (Scheduled Re-inspection / Growth Monitoring)
AcceptableERF < 0.80 (Safe Operation within Standard Margins)

Note on Mechanical Dents: ERF (Estimated Repair Factor) is defined specifically for pressure containment reduction in metal-loss / wall thinning anomalies (ASME B31G). Dents are geometric deformation anomalies governed by ASME B31.8 / B31.4 allowable depth limits (%OD); therefore, ERF is Not Applicable (N/A) for dents.

08 Reliability & Assessment Modes

Deterministic Mode: Nominal calculations applying design safety factor (Psafe = Pf / SF).

Probabilistic Mode: Executes a 5,000-iteration Monte Carlo reliability simulation with mutually independent random variates for anomaly depth (z0), flaw dimension (z1), and material yield strength (z2). Unclamped through-wall occurrences (dsim ≥ tnom) immediately trigger containment failure (Pf = 0).

09 Corrosion Growth Rate

Historical inspection depths are normalized against nominal wall thickness to calculate corrosion growth rate (mm/year) with sub-day calendar precision:

Depth CGR(Current d_mm − Historical d_mm) / Years Elapsed
Life to 80% WT(0.80 · t_nom − Current reported d_mm) / (Depth CGR in mm/yr)
Life to ERF 1.0Iterative projection until Safe Burst Pressure drops below MAOP
